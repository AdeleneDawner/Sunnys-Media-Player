    Settings (this device only)

    Record each cue in your own voice (Chrome will ask for the microphone the first time), close to the mic in a quiet room. Or use Load file for a recording made elsewhere, e.g. a phone voice memo. Recordings play instantly; text-to-speech can lag. Background hiss between words is removed and the level is evened out automatically.
    to · everything off by
    At the start time, music stops with "all done" (a podcast is left to finish). Until the end time, music and starting a podcast get "not doing that". At the "everything off by" time, whatever is still playing stops.
